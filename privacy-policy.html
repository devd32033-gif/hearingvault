Privacy Policy

HearingVault is a case management app for advocates. Your case records stay in the app's private storage on your phone; a copy reaches the cloud only when you sign in and sync. This page explains exactly what is stored, where, for how long, how our advertising is verified, and how to delete everything.

  • Effective 2 December 2025
  • Last updated 29 October 2026
  • Applies to HearingVault 1.15 and later

Device first

Cases, hearings, fees and attached documents live in the app's private storage on your phone.

Cloud only if you sign in

Signed-in users get a copy of their case records in their own Firebase account, keyed to their user ID.

Ads, not ad tracking profiles

Ads come from Google AdMob and are verified with app-ads.txt. Case data is never passed to the ad SDK.

One-tap deletion

Settings → Delete Account removes the cloud records, the device files and the login itself.

1Who we are

HearingVault is published by AACrazy Enterprises (India). In this policy "we", "us" and "our" mean AACrazy Enterprises, and "the app" means the HearingVault Android application distributed on Google Play under the package name com.aacrazyenterprises.hearingvault.

We are the data controller for the personal data described here. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the same contact point below handles data protection requests. Our contact address is aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com.

2Data we collect

2.1 Information you enter or import

  • Account details: email address, display name and the sign-in method (email and password, or Google Sign-In). If you use Google Sign-In we receive your email, name and account identifier from Google. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Case records: case title, case number, court, client name, client contact number, case type, status, notes and important dates.
  • Hearings: hearing dates, purpose, court room, outcome and notes.
  • Fee and payment entries: amounts, dates, payment mode and remarks.
  • Documents you attach: PDFs and images you pick from your device, plus PDFs the app generates (drafts, reports) and files you export.
  • Optional AI drafting input: the command text you type and the draft produced, when you use online AI drafting with your own API key (see section 5).
  • App lock credentials: the PIN you set is stored only as a one-way SHA-256 hash on your device. Biometric unlock uses Android's own biometric prompt; we never receive your fingerprint or face data.

2.2 Information created automatically

  • Firebase Analytics: app launches, feature and screen usage events, app version, device model, operating system version, language, app instance identifier, and a coarse region derived from your IP address. We use this to see which screens fail or go unused — not to build a profile of you.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash stack traces, the state of the app at the time of the crash, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier.
  • Google AdMob (advertising): your advertising ID, IP address (from which a coarse, city-level location can be inferred), device and app information, and ad interaction events such as impressions and clicks. See section 6.
  • Network state: whether you are online, so the app can queue sync and show the offline banner.

4Where your data lives

  • On your device (always): the app keeps its own database and its document folder in private storage that other apps cannot read. This works without an account and without internet.
  • In your Firebase account (only if you sign in and sync): your case records, hearings and fee entries are written to Cloud Firestore under your user ID. Security rules on the server allow a signed-in user to read and write only their own documents.
  • Your attached documents are never uploaded. Files stay in the app's private folder on your phone. This is deliberate: an order sheet is often more sensitive than a case title.
  • Signing out stops syncing. Records already on the device remain available on the device until you delete them.

5AI drafting and translation

AI is optional, and the app works fully without it. It has two distinct modes.

5.1 Online drafting with your own key (opt-in)

If you paste your own Google AI Studio API key into Settings, the advertising-free drafting screen lets you write a draft, a summary or a translation refinement using the Google Gemini API. The request is sent directly from your phone to generativelanguage.googleapis.com; it does not pass through a server of ours.

  • What is sent: the text in your command box, the case details you include in it, and the model name.
  • What comes back: the generated text, which is stored only in the app.
  • The key is stored in the app's encrypted preferences, bound to your device's keystore, and is never written to Firestore or to any log.

Use it with care. On Google's free tier, prompts and responses may be used to improve Google's products, and human reviewers may read them. Do not type client names, addresses, phone numbers or other identifying details into the command box. Type "the client" instead of a name, and edit the draft afterwards.

5.2 On-device features (nothing leaves the phone)

  • Order-sheet OCR (ML Kit text recognition) reads a photo or scan and extracts the text locally.
  • Offline translation (ML Kit Translate) translates drafts on the device. The first use of a language downloads a small model over Wi-Fi; after that it works with no internet.
  • Extracted text, translations and drafts are handled entirely on your device.

6.2 How our ads are verified (app-ads.txt)

Google's Authorized Digital Sellers standard (app-ads.txt) is how an ad exchange confirms that a given app is really allowed to sell inventory under a publisher account. We publish the file at the root of the website listed as our developer website on Google Play, so Google can review it and periodically re-check it:

https://devd32033-gif.github.io/hearingvault/app-ads.txt

The complete contents of that file are:

google.com, pub-2698531881286039, DIRECT, f08c47fec0942fa0
  • google.com — the authorising ad system.
  • pub-2698531881286039 — our AdMob publisher account; it matches the publisher ID in the app's AdMob app ID.
  • DIRECT — we sell this inventory directly, not through a reseller.
  • f08c47fec0942fa0 — Google's certification authority identifier.

Anyone can check the file: if a third party claimed to represent HearingVault's ad inventory, this file would contradict the claim. If the URL above ever stops working, email us — verification also depends on the developer website field in our Play Store listing.

6.3 What the ad SDK can see, and what it cannot

The AdMob SDK can receive, for the purpose of serving and measuring ads:

  • your advertising ID (a resettable identifier controlled in Android settings);
  • your IP address, from which a coarse location can be inferred;
  • device model, operating system, screen size, language, app version and app ID;
  • ad events: request, fill, impression, click, close.

Never shared with the ad SDK: case titles, client names, client phone numbers, notes, hearing details, fee amounts, document contents, your email address, or the text you type into the AI drafting screen. Ads are not built from your case content, and we do not relay your account identity to the ad network.

6.4 Consent

On first launch, where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message through the User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. Ads are only requested after consent has been established.

HearingVault is a professional tool for adult legal practitioners, so the app is not tagged as being directed at children and ads are not served to child-directed audiences.

6.5 Your controls over advertising

  1. Consent form: shown at first launch where required. Choose "non-personalised" if you prefer ads that are not based on your advertising ID.
  2. Device-level advertising ID: open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by manufacturer) and delete or reset the advertising ID. Ads will still appear, but they can no longer be personalised with it.
  3. Remove ads entirely: buy the ad-free subscription (hearing_vault_ad_free_151218) from Settings → Ad-Free. Once it is active the app stops requesting banners and interstitials, and existing ads close. Cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  4. Ask us: email aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com if you want help withdrawing a consent choice or understanding what an ad was.
  5. Report a bad ad: send us a screenshot; we block categories and individual advertisers through AdMob's blocking controls.

Google's own description of how it uses information from apps that use its services is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

7.2 Deleting your account and data

Deleting your account deletes your data, not just your login. The in-app flow runs the steps below in order, and stops with an error if the cloud step fails, so you can retry instead of ending up with orphaned records.

  1. Open Settings Sign in, open the app drawer menu and choose Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account It is in the account section at the bottom of the page and is labelled in red.
  3. Confirm you are the account holder Re-enter your password, or re-authenticate with Google. This prevents someone holding an unlocked phone from wiping your practice records.
  4. Deletion runs All your Firestore records (cases, hearings, payments) are deleted, then the document files on the device, then the local database, then the Firebase login itself. You are returned to the sign-in screen.

7.3 What exactly is erased

  • Every cloud record tied to your user ID: cases, hearings, fee and payment entries.
  • Every attached PDF and image, and every PDF the app generated, in the app's private folders.
  • The local database, so no case remains readable on the phone.
  • Your sign-in details as stored by the app, and the Firebase Authentication account itself.
  • The ad-free entitlement record is tied to your Google Play account, not to the deleted login.

7.4 What is not erased

  • Aggregated, de-identified analytics that can no longer be linked to you.
  • Crash reports already sent, which expire on their own schedule (90 days).
  • Purchase records held by Google Play for accounting and tax purposes.
  • Anything we are legally required to keep, and anything needed to defend a legal claim.
  • Copies of records inside Google's infrastructure backups, which are overwritten on their normal rotation.

7.5 If you cannot use the app

Follow the request flow on the Account Deletion page, or email aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com from the address registered to the account. We verify ownership, then delete the account and its cloud records, and confirm by email. Such requests are completed within 30 days, usually much sooner. The device copy disappears when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

8Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS. The app is configured to refuse cleartext connections.
  • Cloud records are protected by server-side Firestore rules: a signed-in user can only read and write documents that carry their own user ID.
  • Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never stored by the app in readable form.
  • The app-lock PIN is stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash; biometric unlock uses the Android biometric prompt, so fingerprint and face data never leave the device's secure hardware.
  • Your AI API key is kept in keystore-backed encrypted preferences, is never logged, and is displayed masked on screen.
  • Access to the Firebase project is limited to the developer account and protected by multi-factor sign-in.

No system is completely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, email us immediately at aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users and the competent authority as required by law.

10Sharing, disclosure and sale

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for cross-app advertising profiles of our own. Data is disclosed only:

  • to the service providers listed in section 9, strictly to run the features you use;
  • when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid court order — and where we are permitted, we will tell you;
  • to protect rights and safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or to defend a legal claim;
  • in a business transfer, if the app is sold or merged. The buyer would be bound by this policy for data already collected, and we would tell you before your data became subject to a different one;
  • with your direction, for example when you use the app's share or export function to send a document to another app.

Sharing a document or report through Android's share sheet sends it wherever you choose — that destination is outside our control and outside this policy.

11Children's privacy

HearingVault is a professional tool for advocates and is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18, and the app is not tagged as child-directed, so advertisements are not served to a child audience.

12Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where relevant, the GDPR, you may:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data — most fields can be edited directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and data, in the app or by request (section 7).
  • Export a copy of your records — ask us and we will provide a machine-readable file.
  • Withdraw consent to advertising personalisation at any time, without losing access to the app.
  • Object or restrict processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Complain to your supervisory authority — the Data Protection Board of India, or your EU/UK data protection authority.

Email us to exercise any of these rights. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify that you control the account, so that one person cannot request another's data.
