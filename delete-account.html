Legal · Data deletion

Delete your account and data

You can delete HearingVault and everything in it at any time. This page lists the in-app route, the web route and the email route, and exactly what each one removes.

  • No questions asked
  • Cloud records removed
  • Email requests completed within 30 days

Fastest route

Settings → Delete Account inside the app. It runs immediately, with no waiting period.

What goes

Cases, hearings, fee entries, documents, local database, app sign-in and the account itself.

What stays

Aggregated analytics, crash reports until they expire, and Google Play purchase history.

1Delete from inside the app (recommended)

This is the most secure method, because you prove you own the account as part of the flow.

  1. Open HearingVault and sign in If the account is already signed in, the app opens on the dashboard.
  2. Open Settings Use the menu on the dashboard and choose Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account It sits in the account section at the bottom of the page, in red.
  4. Confirm your identity Re-enter your password, or re-authenticate with Google if you signed in with Google.
  5. Wait for the confirmation The app deletes your cloud records, then the device files, then the local database, then the login. You are returned to the sign-in screen.

This cannot be undone. There is no recovery window and no restore from our side. Export or share anything you need before you confirm.

If the cloud step fails — no connection, for example — the app stops and tells you, and nothing is deleted yet. Retry when you are back online. This is deliberate: it prevents half-deleted accounts with orphaned records.

If you only want to remove some records

You do not need to delete the account to remove data. Individual cases, hearings, fee entries and documents can be deleted from the case detail screen, and clearing the app's storage in Android settings removes everything held on the device without touching the cloud copy.

3Request deletion by email

Use this route if you have lost access to the app or cannot sign in. For security, the request must come from the email address registered to the account.

  1. Send the request Email aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
  2. We verify ownership We may ask you to confirm a detail only the account holder would know, or to reply from the address on file if you wrote from another one.
  3. We delete and confirm The account and all its cloud records are deleted, and we reply to confirm. This is completed within 30 days, and usually within a few working days.

The copy on your phone is untouched by an email request — it disappears when you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android settings.

4What survives deletion

  • Aggregated analytics that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Crash reports already submitted, which expire after 90 days.
  • Google Play purchase records — order history, invoices and tax records are held by Google, not by us. An active ad-free subscription is a Google Play subscription, so cancel it in the Play Store to stop future charges.
  • Records we must keep by law, and what is needed to defend a legal claim.
  • Copies in infrastructure backups, which are overwritten on their normal rotation.

Deleting your HearingVault account does not cancel an ad-free subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions first, then delete the account.

5If something blocks you

Forgot the password Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen, reset it through the email link, then delete from Settings.
Lost access to the email address Email us and describe the account; we will verify you by other means and delete it.
Already uninstalled the app The device copy is gone. Install the app again to sign in and delete, or send an email request.
Deletion stopped with an error Usually a network problem. Go online and repeat Settings → Delete Account; nothing is removed until every step succeeds.
You signed in with Google Confirm with the Google account picker. We never see your Google password.

6Questions about deletion

Email aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com and put "Deletion" in the subject. We reply within 7 working days. The full explanation of what the app stores, and how our advertising is verified, is in the privacy policy.
